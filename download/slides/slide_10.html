FleetVision AI — Zeroth Review Presentation
FleetVision AI | Zeroth Review 10
To close, I want to highlight four key differentiators that set FleetVision AI apart. First, this is not just another CRUD application — it combines comprehensive enterprise fleet management with seven practical AI modules powered by real machine learning algorithms including XGBoost and Scikit-learn. Second, the project demonstrates true full-stack expertise, spanning a React frontend, Spring Boot backend, Python AI microservices, Prisma ORM, and well-designed REST APIs. Third, every feature is grounded in real-world impact — we are addressing genuine problems that logistics and fleet companies face daily, with measurable outcomes like 15 to 20 percent fuel cost reduction and 85 percent predictive maintenance accuracy. Fourth, our AI integration goes far beyond simple dashboards and charts — we deliver predictive maintenance, real-time driver behavior analysis, intelligent route optimization, and even accident risk prediction. This project serves both as a rigorous academic capstone and as a compelling portfolio piece that showcases end-to-end software engineering capability. Thank you for your time and attention.
