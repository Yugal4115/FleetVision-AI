Welcome everyone to the Zeroth Review Presentation of FleetVision AI. FleetVision AI is an intelligent fleet management and predictive analytics platform designed to solve real-world operational challenges faced by logistics and transportation companies. This project represents a full-stack capstone effort that combines enterprise-grade fleet management with seven practical AI modules using real machine learning algorithms. Our team has built a comprehensive system covering everything from vehicle and driver management to trip tracking, fuel logging, maintenance scheduling, and advanced AI-powered predictions. Over the next few slides, we will walk you through the problem statement we are addressing, our core objectives, the complete module suite, the AI differentiators, the system architecture, implementation roadmap, and the expected outcomes.
