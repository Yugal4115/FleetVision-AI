FleetVision AI | Zeroth Review 09
This slide presents a clear comparison between what FleetVision AI delivers in its current scope and where we envision the platform heading. On the left, our six expected outcomes are all backed by measurable targets. We project a 15 to 20 percent reduction in fuel costs through AI-driven route optimization and driver coaching. Predictive maintenance targets 85 percent accuracy in forecasting breakdowns before they happen. Real-time driver behavior analysis will improve safety across the fleet. Fleet utilization should improve significantly as we reduce vehicle idle time from 30 percent down to roughly 10 percent. Beyond that, the platform provides centralized operational visibility and empowers data-driven decision-making through AI-powered dashboards. On the right side, we outline six future enhancements that extend the platform's capabilities. These include IoT integration via OBD-II for live engine diagnostics, live GPS tracking with geofencing, AI-based driver drowsiness detection using camera feeds, carbon emission and sustainability analytics, electric vehicle battery monitoring, and dedicated mobile applications for both fleet managers and drivers. These enhancements represent a natural evolution of the platform and position it for long-term industry relevance.
