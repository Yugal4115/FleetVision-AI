FleetVision AI | Zeroth Review 04
FleetVision AI is built as a complete fleet management suite composed of nine tightly integrated modules. Let me give you a quick tour. User Management handles registration, JWT-based authentication, role-based access control so admins, managers, and drivers each see only what they need, and profile management. Vehicle Management is the digital registry for every vehicle in the fleet — tracking documents, real-time status, and odometer readings over time. Driver Management covers license tracking with expiry warnings, performance ratings derived from AI behavior analysis, vehicle-to-driver assignment, and emergency contact information. Trip Management lets dispatchers plan routes, track trip costs in real time, update trip status, and monitor estimated time of arrival. Fuel Management provides a logging system for every fill-up, mileage-per-gallon tracking, cost analysis across the fleet, and monthly fuel reports. Maintenance Scheduling shifts the organization from reactive repairs to preventive servicing — tracking service history, repair records, cost analysis, and generating upcoming service alerts. Expense Tracking aggregates costs across multiple categories including vehicle-specific and driver-specific expenses with monthly reporting. Notifications is the proactive alerting engine that warns about insurance expiry, license renewal deadlines, upcoming service due dates, and real-time breakdown alerts. Finally, Reports and Analytics is the intelligence layer — generating driver performance reports, vehicle utilization reports, and supporting export to both PDF and Excel for offline analysis and custom reporting needs. Together, these nine modules form an end-to-end fleet management ecosystem where every piece of data flows into actionable insights.
