FleetVision AI | Zeroth Review 05
This slide highlights the seven AI-powered intelligence layers that differentiate FleetVision AI from traditional fleet management systems. Each module leverages real machine learning algorithms—primarily XGBoost and Scikit-learn—to deliver actionable predictions. Predictive Maintenance uses historical data including mileage, engine temperature, and oil consumption patterns to forecast component failures with 85% accuracy. This allows fleet managers to schedule maintenance proactively rather than reactively, preventing costly roadside breakdowns. Driver Behavior Analysis monitors driving patterns in real-time—overspeeding, harsh braking, sharp turns—and assigns a safety score to each driver. This enables targeted coaching and reduces accident risk across the fleet. Fuel Prediction models anticipate weekly and monthly fuel requirements based on planned routes and historical consumption, achieving a projected 20% reduction in fuel costs. Route Optimization finds the most efficient paths by analyzing traffic, distance, and delivery priorities, saving an estimated 15% of trip time. Accident Risk Prediction evaluates weather data, driver history, and vehicle condition to generate a risk score from 0 to 100, enabling proactive safety interventions. Vehicle Health Score tracks five key components to provide a holistic health rating per vehicle. Finally, Fleet Analytics synthesizes all module data into business intelligence dashboards that empower data-driven decision-making.
