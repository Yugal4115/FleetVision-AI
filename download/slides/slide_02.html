Let us walk through the core challenges that fleet operators face every day. First, high fuel expenses — diesel and petrol costs are volatile and represent up to forty percent of total operating costs for most logistics companies, yet most operators lack the tools to identify wastage patterns. Second, unexpected breakdowns — when a truck or delivery van fails on the road, it does not just mean repair bills; it means missed delivery windows, unhappy customers, and cascading delays across the supply chain. Third, unsafe driving — without systematic monitoring, speeding, harsh braking, and reckless cornering go undetected until an accident happens. Fourth, poor route planning — dispatchers still rely on manual judgment and experience, leading to suboptimal routes that waste both fuel and driver hours. Fifth, manual maintenance — most fleets follow a reactive repair model where vehicles are fixed only after they break down, rather than a preventive approach that could have caught the issue early. Sixth, no centralized monitoring — data lives in scattered spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and paper logbooks, making it impossible to get a holistic view of operations. Seventh, document expiry — insurance policies, driver licenses, and vehicle registration certificates go unnoticed until a violation occurs. Eighth, no performance insights — fleet managers cannot answer basic questions like which driver is most efficient, which vehicle is most underutilized, or where the biggest cost leaks are. These eight challenges collectively cost the logistics industry billions of dollars annually, and FleetVision AI is built to address every single one of them.
